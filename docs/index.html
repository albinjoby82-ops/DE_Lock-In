DE Lock-In

Digital Electronics mid-term, Lectures 1 to 10. Learn it by doing it.

How to use it

  1. Notes first. Each idea is a short rule, a diagram, then a question. Answer on paper before you open the answer.
  2. Then the quiz. Fill K-maps, type expressions, pick answers. It checks your working and tells you why.
  3. Then mix it. The mixed drill throws topics at you in random order, like the exam.
