DE Lock-In

Digital Electronics mid-term, Lectures 1 to 10. Learn it by doing it.

How to use it

  1. Notes first. Each idea is a short rule, a diagram, then a question. Answer on paper before you open the answer.
  2. Then the quiz. Fill K-maps, type expressions, pick answers. It checks your working and tells you why.
  3. Then mix it. The mixed drill throws topics at you in random order, like the exam.
  4. Then the book. Work the Mano problems for the topic on paper, then sit a timed mock mid-term.

Topics

Book questions

End-of-chapter problems from Mano & Ciletti, Digital Design (4th ed.), each with a full worked solution. Every topic is split into a few skills; each skill has 1 to 4 core questions and the rest is optional extra practice. Short on time? Do the Core path.

Mock mid-term

Random book questions against the clock. Typed answers are checked; paper answers you mark yourself.
